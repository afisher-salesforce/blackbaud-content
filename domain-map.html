Salesforce Domain Map

This domain view translates capability taxonomy into business meaning, so Blackbaud stakeholders can prioritize what to activate now, what to fund next, and what to evaluate as optional overlays.

Acronym note: capability codes are shorthand only. Decision-making should use capability names, business outcomes, and contract status context.

Data and AI Contracted backbone

Business purpose: Create trusted, context-rich recommendations and AI actions grounded in enterprise data.

  • Priority capabilities: DAG Agentic AI / Autonomous Agents, DDH Data Harmonization, DVS Vector Search and RAG Grounding.
  • Why it matters for Blackbaud: Delivers relevance and explainability for content recommendations without compromising trust controls.

Sales Contracted and operationally critical

Business purpose: Improve seller effectiveness with guided content and deal-stage alignment.

  • Priority capabilities: SEM Sales Enablement, SGS Guided Solution Selling, SDS Deal Support Requests.
  • Why it matters for Blackbaud: Converts content strategy into frontline behavior and measurable pipeline movement.

Marketing Mostly contracted, selective expansion

Business purpose: Govern lifecycle, targeting, and distribution of GTM content across audiences.

  • Priority capabilities: MCN Content Management, MAS Audience Segmentation and Activation, MMA Marketing Analytics.
  • Why it matters for Blackbaud: Reduces duplication and strengthens alignment between marketing content supply and sales demand.

Integration Contracted dependency layer

Business purpose: Connect content systems, identity sources, and workflow surfaces without brittle custom architecture.

  • Priority capabilities: IMS Microsoft Ecosystem Integration, IEI Enterprise Integration, IEP Enterprise Platform Connectors.
  • Why it matters for Blackbaud: Enables Teams-first delivery while preserving Salesforce as the intelligence control plane.

Analytics and Reporting Contracted decision layer

Business purpose: Tie content consumption and seller behavior to revenue outcomes and prioritization decisions.

  • Priority capabilities: ABI Embedded BI and Dashboards, SSA Sales Analytics, AEX Einstein Conversation Insights.
  • Why it matters for Blackbaud: Creates an objective basis for what content to scale, retire, or redesign.

Platform and Governance Contracted trust layer

Business purpose: Maintain privacy, security, and operating-model controls as AI-driven content usage scales.

  • Priority capabilities: PGV Data Governance and Privacy, PMD Multi-Org and Subsidiary Architecture.
  • Why it matters for Blackbaud: Preserves policy integrity and legal defensibility across every activation phase.

Advisory Readout for Leadership

What to Activate First

Prioritize Data and AI + Sales + Integration to improve seller effectiveness quickly while retaining governance controls.

What to Evaluate Selectively

Use ISV overlays only for validated gaps (Gong-native workflows and buyer engagement telemetry), not as a replacement architecture.

What to Measure Monthly

Track recommendation adoption, no-result search terms, content influence on opportunities, and governance SLA adherence.
