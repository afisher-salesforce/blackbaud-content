5. Security, Trust, and Adoption

Support AI-enabled scale with enterprise trust controls, compliance posture, and adoption mechanisms that hold under scrutiny.

Requirement cluster: GDPR/CCPA controls, encryption, AI boundaries, onboarding, managed advisory

Current State

Contracted platform controls are strong, but executive confidence depends on consistent policy activation and visibility.

Future State

Governance by design: trust-layer protections, policy enforcement, and adoption telemetry are built into every content workflow.

Value Driver

Lower compliance risk and faster executive approval for AI-led operational expansion.

Capabilities

DEL: Einstein Trust Layer

Applies masking, logging, and third-party model retention controls across AI interactions.

PGV: Data Governance and Privacy

Maintains compliance boundaries and auditable control points across data domains.

VKB: Knowledge Management

Institutionalizes decision support knowledge for field and leadership teams.

VLN: Training and Onboarding

Improves behavioral adoption and reinforces trusted use of AI-guided workflows.
