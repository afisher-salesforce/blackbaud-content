Company and Market Position

Blackbaud is a leading social impact software provider with broad global footprint, high switching costs, and deep domain credibility across nonprofit and education segments.

FY2025 revenue is approximately $1.12B with GAAP profitability achieved after portfolio refocus and EVERFI divestiture. Recurring model strength supports strategic reinvestment.

AI Direction

Agents for Good and expanded AI capability signals indicate leadership intent to move from systems of record toward systems of intelligent action.

Competitive Dynamics

Salesforce, Microsoft, and specialized enablement entrants are converging around workflow intelligence. Differentiation will come from execution model and governance maturity.

Salesforce-Relevant Implication

The strongest path is not net-new platform replacement; it is maximizing existing Salesforce contracts as the governed backbone while selectively closing workflow gaps.
