Blackbaud Content Management Rationalization

Salesforce Executive Discussion Site

Operating Context

Blackbaud is balancing AI-led growth ambition, post-divestiture focus, and a requirement to simplify GTM execution while protecting control points in data, trust, and seller productivity.

Market context and strategic implications from External Research Brief

Current State

Blackbaud operates at scale in social impact software with strong recurring revenue and deep incumbent workflows, while managing competitive pressure from platform alternatives and specialized enablement tools.

Future State

A unified content operating model where CRM, AI, and collaboration signals produce guided recommendations in the seller flow, backed by governed multi-channel distribution.

Value Driver

Faster cycle time from insight to action: less time searching, fewer duplicated assets, and stronger linkage between content decisions and pipeline outcomes.

Priority Discussion Threads

1) Replace Point-Solution Fragmentation

Rationalize overlapping content tooling while preserving in-workflow usability where adoption risk is highest.

2) Scale Trustworthy AI

Expand recommendation intelligence under clear governance controls and legal defensibility.

3) Prove Revenue Impact

Establish consistent attribution from content usage to opportunity movement and close performance.
