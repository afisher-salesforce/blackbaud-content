2. In-Workflow Seller Experience

Improve seller adoption by aligning recommendations to the actual flow of work, especially in Gong and Teams-driven behaviors.

Requirement cluster: role/context recommendations, Gong-centric UX, and guidance quality

Current State

Salesforce can generate high-quality guidance, but experience friction appears where recommendations are not surfaced in the primary seller environment.

Future State

A blended motion where Salesforce powers intelligence and governance while the final interaction surface follows seller behavior patterns.

Value Driver

Higher adoption, less rep friction, and better conversion from insight to deal action.

Capabilities

Sales Enablement (SEM)

Curates competitive, stage, and persona-relevant assets for deal execution.

Guided Solution Selling (SGS)

Improves message consistency and reduces cycle-time variability across teams.

Vector Search and RAG Grounding (DVS)

Enables fast semantic retrieval with rationale and source context.

Agentic AI / Autonomous Agents (DAG)

Delivers contextual recommendations into user flow with governance intact.
