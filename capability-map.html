Capability Sequencing

This sequencing model translates capability investment into executive decisions, so Blackbaud can retire Seismic risk while improving seller adoption, governance, and measurable revenue impact.

How to read this page: each phase shows business outcome, capability names in plain language, dependencies, and an executive decision required to proceed.

Phase 1: Foundation and Flow (0-90 days)

Primary goal Stand up trusted content intelligence in the current seller flow.

  • Business outcome: Sellers get reliable, role-aware recommendations in Teams and CRM without adding workflow friction.
  • Key capabilities: DDH Data Harmonization, IMS Microsoft Ecosystem Integration, MCN Content Management, SEM Sales Enablement, DEL Einstein Trust Layer.
  • Dependencies: SharePoint ingestion rules, permissions model alignment, and core taxonomy ownership.
  • Requirement alignment: Req 1.1, 1.4, 2.1, 3.1, 5.3.
  • Decision now: Approve Salesforce as the governed intelligence backbone and limit overlay tools to targeted workflow gaps.

Phase 2: Measurement and Scale (90-180 days)

Primary goal Link content behavior to revenue outcomes and adoption quality.

  • Business outcome: Leadership can prioritize content and process investments using objective performance signals.
  • Key capabilities: ABI Embedded BI and Dashboards, SSA Sales Analytics, MMA Marketing Analytics, SDS Deal Support Requests, DVS Vector Search and RAG Grounding.
  • Dependencies: No-result search logging, dashboard governance, and consistent opportunity attribution logic.
  • Requirement alignment: Req 3.7, 4.1, 4.2, 4.4.
  • Decision now: Set executive KPI definitions and commit to monthly operating reviews for content performance.

Phase 3: Expansion and Precision (180+ days)

Primary goal Extend governed access and close remaining high-value workflow gaps.

  • Business outcome: Non-CRM GTM users and buyer-facing motions gain controlled access without fragmenting governance.
  • Key capabilities: ORE Recommendations and Experience, VSS Self Service, plus selected ISV layers for Gong-native and buyer-engagement gaps.
  • Dependencies: Experience Cloud funding decision and clear ISV selection criteria tied to quantified value.
  • Requirement alignment: Req 2.2, 3.2, 4.3.
  • Decision now: Approve a controlled “complement, not replace” policy for Spekit/ISV evaluation.

Executive Validation Lens

1) Value Protection

Are we maximizing already-contracted Salesforce capabilities before introducing incremental software spend?

2) Flow-of-Work Adoption

Does each phase improve the seller experience in current behavior patterns (especially Gong + Teams) rather than force a net-new motion?

3) Governance Integrity

Do trust, privacy, and analytics accountability remain anchored in Salesforce even when complementary tools are added?
