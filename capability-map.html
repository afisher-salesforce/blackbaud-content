Capability Sequencing

A phased sequence prioritizes business urgency, dependency order, time-to-value, and risk containment while protecting existing contract value.

Coverage signal from capability assessment: 27 contracted, 2 pipeline, 1 evaluating, 1 pending

Phase 1: Foundation and Flow

Focus: DDH, IMS, MCN, SEM, SGS, DEL

Establish harmonized ingestion, recommendation relevance, and policy-safe execution inside current operating channels.

Phase 2: Measurement and Scale

Focus: ABI, SSA, MMA, SDS, DVS

Operationalize attribution and adoption telemetry, then routinize continuous optimization loops.

Phase 3: Expansion and Precision

Focus: ORE, VSS, selected ISV overlays

Extend role-based experiences for non-CRM personas and selectively close residual workflow gaps.

Decision Notes for Rationalization

Retire Seismic Risk

Use Salesforce as default platform backbone; only add overlays where in-workflow behavior cannot be met natively.

Avoid False Binary

Frame Spekit and other enablement tools as optional experience layers rather than replacement architectures.

Protect Outcome Governance

Keep trust, data policy, and analytics controls anchored in Salesforce to avoid fragmented accountability.
