Executive Summary

This perspective is designed for Blackbaud and Salesforce leadership to prioritize capability adoption that retires Seismic risk, preserves flow-of-work, and accelerates measurable GTM outcomes.

Prepared for pre-sales strategy discussion | Audience: Executive, Revenue, Operations, and Technology leaders

Strategic Position

Blackbaud already owns the majority of platform components needed for a modern content management operating model. The highest-value decision now is sequencing: where Salesforce runs as the intelligence backbone and where complementary layers should be used only when they close clear in-workflow gaps.

Coverage Signal

85% aligned

Salesforce addresses most requirements through contracted capabilities, with targeted gaps centered on Gong-native surfacing and buyer-side engagement telemetry.

Commercial Signal

Contract value protection

Agentforce, Data Cloud, MuleSoft, Tableau, Slack, and Shield are already in contract through January 2029, enabling value capture without platform replacement risk.

Decision Signal

Rationalization play

Position Salesforce as the system-of-intelligence and governance layer; evaluate additive workflow accelerators only where they reduce friction in Gong-centered behavior.

Executive Stakes by Persona

CRO / Revenue Leadership

Increase decision velocity and win-rate consistency by aligning content to opportunity stage, role, and call signals without introducing fragmented seller workflows.

CIO / CTO

Reduce architecture sprawl by consolidating intelligence, governance, and security controls on platforms already under enterprise contract.

RevOps and Enablement

Standardize approval, distribution, and analytics processes while preserving local flexibility for teams that operate in Microsoft 365 and Gong.

Data, AI, and Risk Leaders

Scale AI-assisted recommendations under trust-layer controls, clear auditability, and policy-driven data boundaries.
