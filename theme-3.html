3. Content Governance and Distribution

Establish an enterprise content operating model that controls quality, permissions, and lifecycle while enabling rapid multichannel distribution.

Requirement cluster: approvals, lifecycle controls, tagging, role-based access, and distribution

Current State

Governance mechanisms exist, but value depends on disciplined process design and clear ownership across GTM functions.

Future State

Submission-to-publication paths are policy-driven, measurable, and integrated with collaboration channels to reduce operational friction.

Value Driver

Lower governance risk, faster approvals, and consistent customer-facing messaging.

Capabilities

MCN: Content Management

Provides governed creation, versioning, and publication controls across channels.

SDS: Deal Support Requests

Routes high-value requests to specialists with accountability and SLA transparency.

ORE: Recommendations and Experience

Supports personalized delivery experiences for broader GTM personas.

VSS: Self Service

Extends controlled access for non-CRM users through purpose-built entry points.
