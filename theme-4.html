Salesforce Executive Discussion Site

4. Intelligence and Attribution

Translate content behavior into executive decision signals by connecting usage, engagement, and revenue movement.

Requirement cluster: seller analytics, marketing attribution, no-result query reporting, executive dashboards

Current State

Leadership can measure activity, but buyer-level engagement visibility and full attribution depth require targeted design choices.

Future State

A single measurement model connects content discovery, seller behavior, and opportunity outcomes for faster portfolio decisions.

Value Driver

Improved investment discipline through measurable impact and clear content retirement decisions.

Capabilities

Embedded BI and Dashboards (ABI)

Places strategic and operational insight directly in the flow of execution.

Sales Analytics (SSA)

Monitors rep behavior and opportunity influence from enablement interactions.

Marketing Analytics (MMA)

Evaluates campaign and content contribution against pipeline and revenue goals.

Vector Search and RAG Grounding (DVS)

Logs retrieval demand patterns, including no-result queries, to direct portfolio refinement.
